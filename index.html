Who I Am

The story so far

My path here wasn't exactly linear, and I like it that way. I started in hospitality — studying guest experience and sales at IHM Pusa — before pivoting hard into data science at IIIT Bengaluru, and later a tech-focused MBA at Masters' Union. Somewhere in between, I built a vegan dessert brand from my kitchen to ₹37L+ in annual revenue, entirely solo. That mix — people-first instincts, a data-driven brain, and a founder's comfort with ambiguity — is what I bring to every growth and strategy problem I touch.

I diagnose before I solve. I've stood up CRM infrastructure alone across three countries, walked 50+ dark stores to find the real bottleneck, and built frameworks that outlasted my time on the project. Whether the problem is "we're losing users" or "we don't even know what the problem is," I like structuring the mess, prioritizing ruthlessly, and shipping something that actually moves the number.
